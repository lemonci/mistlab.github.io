Seyed Ehsan Marjani Bajestani is a Postdoctoral Fellow at the MIST Lab. He
started in robotics by building a line-follower robot in high school, went on to
a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and an M.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering, and
became deeply involved in multi-agent robotics as a member of the Mechatronics
Research Laboratory and an executive committee member of the RoboCup Middle Size
soccer robot League.

## Current Work

Ehsan's research interests are in multi-agent perception — cooperative
localization, tracking, and formation strategies. He currently focuses on
cooperative tracking using event-based vision, across environments ranging from
structured soccer fields to fully unknown terrain.

## Selected Awards

- 1st place, RoboCup 2015, Middle Size League Technical Challenge (Hefei)
- 1st place, IranOpen 2017 RoboCup, Middle Size League (Tehran)
- 3rd place, RoboCup 2016, Middle Size League Technical Challenge (Leipzig)

Off the clock, Ehsan is a big fan of soccer — and still plays.
