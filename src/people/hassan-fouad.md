Hassan Fouad is a Postdoctoral Fellow at the MIST Lab. He received his B.Sc. and
M.Sc. degrees in Aerospace Engineering from Cairo University and completed his
PhD in Computer Engineering at Polytechnique Montréal. His background spans
control and guidance, system modeling and simulation, parameter identification
and estimation, robot networks, and hands-on design and fabrication.

## Current Work

Hassan's research focuses on energy sufficiency and persistence for autonomous
robots — using tools such as control barrier functions to enable long-term
missions in unknown environments.
