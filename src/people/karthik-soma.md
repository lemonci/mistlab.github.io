Karthik Soma is a PhD student at Polytechnique Montréal and Mila Quebec AI Institute, supervised by Prof. Giovanni Beltrame. He holds a B.Tech in Instrument and Control Engineering from the National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli (GPA: 8.98/10.00).

His research spans swarm intelligence, collective decision-making, and multi-agent reinforcement learning. He has published work at top robotics and AI venues including ICRA, TMLR, MRS, DARS, and ECAI.

## Current Work

Karthik's doctoral work focuses on collective intelligence in robot swarms — exploring how large groups of simple robots can make decisions, share resources, and navigate collaboratively. Current projects include:

- **Swarm Biotactics**: Developing collective strategies for centroid estimation, flocking, and navigation using bio-hybrid swarms.
- **Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning**: Benchmarking MARL algorithms in complex coordination tasks with heterogeneous agents, in collaboration with Thales Digital Solutions Inc.
- **The Hive Mind**: Investigating how swarm behavior emerges from a single RL agent trained across a population of robots.
