Haechan Mark Bong is a PhD student in computer engineering at Polytechnique Montréal and MILA, supervised by Professor Giovanni Beltrame. He received his BSc. in computer science and his MSc. in animal science from McGill University. He is interested in the application of foundation models in agricultural robotics.

## Current Work

- **Agricultural robotics** — Application of visual-language-action models for autonomous task completion.
- **Agricultural AI** — Application of open-vocabulary detection methods for generalized crop detection.
