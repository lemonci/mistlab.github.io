Monica Li is a PhD student at the MIST Lab. She received a master's degree in
computer science from Bishop's University in January 2023.

## Current Work

Monica researches 3D Gaussian mapping for multi-robot Simultaneous Localization
and Mapping (SLAM). Her work is carried out in close collaboration with Professor
Daniele De Martini's group at the Oxford Robotics Institute (ORI).
