Phuc Nguyen is a PhD student at Université de Montréal, supervised by Prof. Giovanni Beltrame. His research focuses on enabling agents to collaborate effectively to automate complex tasks. His work encompasses hierarchical reinforcement learning for strategy discovery, coordination in bio-robotic swarms, and asynchronous control of drone systems.

## Current Work

* **Asynchronous Learning**: Research asynchronous deep learning frameworks for scalable and efficient distributed training.
* **Swarm Coordination**: Research unified platforms for collective intelligence, autonomous swarm behavior, and sensor fusion integration.
