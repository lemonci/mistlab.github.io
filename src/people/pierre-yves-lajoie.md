Pierre-Yves Lajoie is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer and
Software Engineering at Polytechnique Montréal. He is a robotics and artificial
intelligence researcher specializing in robotic perception for single- and
multi-agent systems. Before joining Polytechnique, he held research positions at
MIT (2019), the Samsung AI Center (2023), and the University of Oxford (2024).

## Research Interests

- **Spatial AI & perception** — computer vision and 3D scene understanding for
  robots operating in aerial, underground, indoor, and space environments.
- **Collaborative SLAM** — decentralized, communication-efficient, and outlier-
  resilient simultaneous localization and mapping for robotic teams (e.g.
  Swarm-SLAM, DOOR-SLAM).
- **Distributed systems** — coordination and resource-efficient computation
  across autonomous robot fleets.

He has received the Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship and the Best Paper Award
at IEEE ICC 2024.
