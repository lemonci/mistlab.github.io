Riana Gagnon Souleiman is a PhD student at Polytechnique Montréal. Her research focuses on space robotics, with an emphasis on developing heterogeneous multi-robot exploration strategies to improve exploration planning for extraterrestrial environments.

## Current Work

- **Multi-Robot Exploration**: Studying optimal ways to utilize teams of heterogenous robots to explore harsh environmnets.
- **Traversability Analysis**: Utilizing geometric and semantic terrain information to quantify the cost of traversing terrain.
