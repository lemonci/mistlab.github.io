Yann Bouteiller is a Research Associate at the MIST Lab. He completed a dual
M.Sc. degree at Polytechnique Montréal (specializing in Machine Learning and
Operational Research, with a focus on deep neural networks at Mila) and at the
École des Mines de Saint-Étienne, where his engineering degree specialized in
computer science, embedded systems, and microelectronics. He graduated from
Montpellier University in Mathematics and Computer Science.

## Current Work

Yann explores the potential of machine learning in partially and fully
distributed settings, with current interests in model parallelism, transfer
learning, recurrent neural networks, deep reinforcement learning, and the
intersection of reinforcement learning with swarm robotics.
